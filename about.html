Our Story

The Vision of Bharat Vandana

✦

"Bharat Vandana" — a salutation to India — is not just a name. It is the spirit that animates every acre of this remarkable park.

On 18 December 2019, Union Home Minister Amit Shah laid the foundation stone of what was to become India's most ambitious urban park project. Conceived by the Delhi Development Authority (DDA) and shaped by the brilliant minds at architectural firm ARCOP Associates, Bharat Vandana Park represents Prime Minister Modi's vision of "Ease of Living" for urban citizens.

At its heart, the park is a 220-acre celebration of India's "Unity in Diversity" — a physical space where the geography, culture, and architectural genius of every Indian state and territory coexists in harmony. No other park in India attempts what Bharat Vandana Park does: to bring all of India to one address.

The park is now in phased opening (2025 onwards), and is set to become Delhi's — and India's — most iconic public cultural space. It is more than a park. It is a statement about who we are as a nation.

Architecture & Design

Design Inspired by Rangoli & Lotus

✦

The master plan of Bharat Vandana Park is a work of art in itself — a Rangoli pattern fused with the form of India's national flower, the lotus.

🎨
Rangoli Layout
The entire park is laid out as a traditional Indian Rangoli pattern radiating from the central Vandana Sarovar, creating 10 organic petal-shaped zones that flow naturally into each other.
🏺
Harappan Roots
Inspired by the public spaces of ancient Harappan (Indus Valley) civilisation — one of the world's first examples of planned urban greenspaces — dating back over 5,000 years.
🌿
Designed by ARCOP
ARCOP Associates — one of India's premier architectural firms — designed the park's master plan. The structural engineering was handled by Eckersley O'Callaghan.
🌉
Inspired by Singapore
The iconic skywalk pylon towers are inspired by Singapore's Gardens by the Bay — bringing world-class landscape architecture to Delhi for the first time.
⚙️
Tata Structura Steel
The pylons use Tata Structura high-ductility hollow steel sections specially bent into complex curves without fracturing — an engineering first for any Indian park.
♻️
Sustainability-First
Solar power, 4 rainwater harvesting tanks (10 crore litre capacity), 4 on-site STPs, dual plumbing, and 98 acres of green cover make the park nearly self-sustaining.
History & Timeline

The Journey of Bharat Vandana Park

✦
💡
2018 — Conception
Vision Announced by DDA
The Delhi Development Authority announces plans for a world-class, 200+ acre cultural theme park in Dwarka — to be called Bharat Vandana Park. ARCOP Associates is appointed as the design firm.
🪨
December 18, 2019 — Foundation
Foundation Stone Laid by Amit Shah
Union Home Minister Amit Shah lays the foundation stone of Bharat Vandana Park, formally inaugurating the project. The park's total area is confirmed at 220 acres with ₹524 crore in funding.
🦠
2020–2021 — Pandemic Delay
COVID-19 Halts Construction
The COVID-19 pandemic severely disrupts construction timelines. The originally planned 2022 opening is pushed back. Despite delays, structural work on the pylons and Skywalk continues.
🌳
June 2022 — Green Dispute
Forest Dept Halts Construction Briefly
Delhi's Forest & Wildlife Department issues an injunction after finding trees being cut without proper clearance. Construction is paused. DDA commits to stronger green cover commitments and the issue is resolved.
🔨
2023–2024 — Accelerated Construction
Major Zones Take Shape
Construction accelerates. The Skywalk pylons are completed, Vandana Sarovar takes shape, monument replicas are installed, and the Adventure Zone and Fun Park structures are raised. DDA announces phased opening for 2025.
🎉
2025 — Phased Opening
Park Opens to the Public
Bharat Vandana Park begins phased opening to the public from mid-2025. Initial zones including the Mini India monument trail and Vandana Sarovar are inaugurated. Full opening of all 10 zones is targeted by end 2025.
🌟
2026 — National Landmark
India's Most Iconic Urban Park
Bharat Vandana Park is fully operational and welcoming hundreds of thousands of visitors, establishing itself as Delhi's — and India's — premier cultural and recreational destination.
