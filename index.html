About the Park

A Salutation to India

✦

"Bharat Vandana" — a Sanskrit salutation meaning Salutation to India — is more than a name. It is a promise.

Developed by the Delhi Development Authority (DDA) and designed by the acclaimed architectural firm ARCOP Associates, Bharat Vandana Park is India's most ambitious urban cultural park — a 220-acre living tribute to the nation's extraordinary heritage.

The park's master plan is inspired by ancient Harappan civilisation's tradition of community public spaces, shaped into a Rangoli-petal layout radiating from the central Vandana Sarovar lake — a design that fuses India's oldest civilizational roots with cutting-edge 21st-century landscape architecture.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions

✦
What is Bharat Vandana Park? ▾
Bharat Vandana Park is a 220-acre cultural theme park developed by the Delhi Development Authority (DDA) in Dwarka, Sector 20. Known as "Mini India," it features 36 monument replicas, Vandana Sarovar lake, a 1.2-km Skywalk, adventure zone, and eco-forest — making it India's grandest urban cultural park.
How do I reach the park by metro? ▾
Take the Delhi Metro Blue Line (Line 3) to Dwarka Sector 9 station. The park's main entrance is just 300 metres from the station exit, connected via a dedicated pedestrian walkway.
What is the entry fee for Bharat Vandana Park? ▾
Expected entry fee is ₹50–₹100 for adults and ₹25–₹50 for children. Children under 5 years are likely free. Additional charges apply for adventure zone activities and boating. Confirm current prices at dda.gov.in.
What are the park's opening hours? ▾
The park is expected to be open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays, and until 10:30 PM on weekends and public holidays. The Musical Fountain show is expected at 7:00 PM and 8:30 PM daily.
Is the park family-friendly? ▾
Absolutely! The park features a dedicated Fun Park for children, family boating on Vandana Sarovar, an Animatronic Animal Zoo, and ample green picnic spaces — making it one of Delhi's best family destinations.
What is the best time of year to visit? ▾
October to March (winter) is the ideal season. Delhi's cool climate during this period makes it perfect for outdoor exploration. For the best atmosphere, visit on weekday evenings (6–9 PM) for the Musical Fountain show and illuminated monument replicas.
Is there parking available at the park? ▾
Yes. The park provides on-site parking for approximately 1,500 vehicles including cars, two-wheelers, and buses/coaches. However, we strongly recommend taking the metro (Dwarka Sec 9, 300m walk) to avoid any parking delays on busy weekends.
Who built Bharat Vandana Park? ▾
The park was developed by the Delhi Development Authority (DDA), designed by architectural firm ARCOP Associates, and constructed by NKG Infrastructure and NBCC. The foundation stone was laid on 18 December 2019 by Union Home Minister Amit Shah.
