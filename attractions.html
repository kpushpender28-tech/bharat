Attractions & Zones

Ten thematic zones, 220 acres, and a lifetime of experiences — discover everything that awaits you at Bharat Vandana Park.

01
Heritage Zone

Mini India Zone

The crown jewel of Bharat Vandana Park — a breathtaking walk through India's 5,000-year architectural legacy. Thirty-six scaled monument replicas, grouped into 7 regional clusters, recreate the country's most iconic landmarks using materials authentic to their originals. Each cluster is surrounded by state-specific landscaping and informational panels in Hindi, English, and regional languages.

This is the world's first park where a visitor can walk from the Himalayan temples of the north to the coastal shrines of Tamil Nadu in a single afternoon, experiencing India's remarkable architectural diversity in one magnificent journey.

02
Lake Zone

Vandana Sarovar

At the heart of Bharat Vandana Park lies Vandana Sarovar — a magnificent 26-acre central lake that serves as the visual, ecological, and spiritual focal point of the entire park. Its name means "Lake of Salutation" — a serene mirror reflecting the monuments, sky, and soul of India.

The lake is fed by treated recycled water from the park's four on-site STPs and rainwater harvesting systems, making it an environmentally exemplary water body. A lakeside promenade, viewing platforms, and pedestrian bridges allow visitors to experience the lake from every angle.

03
Aerial Experience

Skywalk & Pylon Towers

One of the most dramatic and visually stunning engineering feats in any Indian city park — the Skywalk is a 1.2-kilometre elevated walkway suspended 18 metres above ground, supported by nine towering pylons each standing 44 metres high.

Inspired by Singapore's iconic Gardens by the Bay Supertrees, the pylons are constructed using Tata Structura high-ductility hollow steel sections, specially engineered to be bent into complex curves without fracturing — a first in Indian park construction. From the Skywalk, visitors enjoy unobstructed 360-degree aerial views of all 10 zones, Vandana Sarovar, and the monument replicas below.

1.2 km Length 18m Height 9 Pylons (44m) Panoramic Views Night Lighting
04
Thrills & Adventure

Adventure Zone

For those who prefer their India experience with a side of adrenaline — the Adventure Zone is Bharat Vandana Park's playground for the bold and the curious. Packed with activities for all ages, this zone transforms a visit to the park into a full-day outdoor adventure experience.

Zip-Lining Tree Surfing Rock Climbing Rope Courses Animatronic Zoo Obstacle Courses

The Animatronic Animal Zoo is a particular favourite — life-like mechanical animals representing India's rich wildlife heritage, from Bengal Tigers to Elephants, create a magical and educational experience for young visitors.

05
Family Fun

Fun Park & Children's Zone

A dedicated wonderland for families and children of all ages, the Fun Park is designed to delight, educate, and entertain simultaneously. All installations are inspired by India's cultural motifs — giving children a playful introduction to their heritage while having the time of their lives.

Amusement Rides Cultural Play Interactive Stations Safe Play Areas Supervised Activities
06
Evening Spectacle

Musical Fountain & Light Show

As the sun dips below the horizon and the monuments are bathed in golden light, Vandana Sarovar transforms into a breathtaking canvas of water, light, and music. The state-of-the-art Musical Fountain is Delhi's most spectacular evening entertainment experience — a synchronized ballet of jets, colour, and classical Indian music.

Shows run daily at 7:00 PM and 8:30 PM. Visitors are encouraged to find a lakeside seat well before show time, especially on weekends.

Daily Shows 7:00 PM & 8:30 PM Light & Water Sync Indian Music Lakeside Seating
07
Peace & Wellness

Meditation Garden & Eco Forest

In the midst of Delhi's relentless urban energy, the Meditation Garden is a sanctuary — a carefully curated space where ancient Indian wisdom meets contemporary landscape design. Here, time slows down, the city's noise fades, and nature speaks in whispers.

Zen Landscaping Yoga Platforms Herb Garden Butterfly Garden Eco Trail Native Plant Species Bird-Friendly Zone

Zones 8, 9 & 10

Zone 8
🎭

Cultural Performance Area

An open-air amphitheatre hosting folk dances, classical music, national festivals, community events, and cultural programmes from every corner of India — a living stage for India's living traditions.

Zone 9
🍽️

Lake View Restaurants

Fine-dining and casual dining restaurants overlooking Vandana Sarovar, serving regional Indian cuisine and multicuisine menus. Food courts and kiosks offering India's favourite street foods are distributed across the park.

Zone 10
🌲

Eco-Sensitive Forest Zone

98 acres of native tree cover — an urban forest within the city. Features biodiversity trails, medicinal plant collections from every Indian state, and bird-watching zones with over 100 recorded species.
