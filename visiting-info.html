Getting Here

How to Reach

✦

Bharat Vandana Park is superbly connected — by metro, road, and bus. We recommend the metro for the most convenient, traffic-free experience.

📍 Sector 20, Dwarka, New Delhi – 110075 | Coordinates: 28.5733° N, 77.0459° E

Open in Google Maps ↗
🚇
By Metro (Recommended)
Nearest Station: Dwarka Sector 9 — Blue Line (Line 3), only 300 metres from the main entrance.

From Rajiv Chowk (CP): Board Blue Line towards Dwarka Sector 21. Alight at Dwarka Sector 9. ~30–35 min journey.

From IGI Airport: Airport Express to Yashobhoomi → Transfer to Blue Line → 2 stops to Dwarka Sector 9.
🚗
By Car / Taxi / Auto
Via Dwarka Expressway (NH-48): Take Sector 20 exit, follow signs to Bharat Vandana Park.
From IGI Airport: ~10–15 km via NH-48, 15–20 min drive.
Cab Services: Ola, Uber, and auto rickshaws serve the area.
On-site parking: 1,500 vehicles. Paid parking available.
🚌
By DTC Bus
Routes: 717C3, 718, 892, D-068, RL-79 stop near Sector 20, Dwarka.
Buses available from Dhaula Kuan, Palam, and ISBT Kashmere Gate.
The Dwarka bus terminal is a convenient interchange point.
✈️
From IGI Airport
The park is just ~10–15 km from Indira Gandhi International Airport — the closest major park to any Indian international airport. By car: 15 min. By Metro (Airport Express + Blue Line): ~25 min. Perfect for transit visitors.
Park Facilities

Everything You Need

✦
🅿️
Parking
On-site paid parking for 1,500+ vehicles including cars, two-wheelers, and tourist coaches.
♿
Accessibility
Wheelchair-accessible pathways, ramps, accessible washrooms, and special viewing areas for persons with disabilities.
🏥
First Aid
First aid stations located at 4 points across the park. Emergency helpline numbers displayed at all entry points.
🍽️
Dining
Lake View Restaurant, multiple food courts, kiosks serving Indian street food and beverages across all 10 zones.
🚻
Restrooms
Clean, well-maintained washroom facilities available at regular intervals across the park. Separate facilities for men, women, and persons with disabilities.
📶
Wi-Fi Zones
Free Wi-Fi available at key zones including the Food Court, Cultural Area, and Visitor Information Centre.
🎒
Cloakroom
Secure cloakroom and luggage storage service available near the main entrance for day visitors.
🛒
Souvenir Shop
Heritage souvenir shops selling miniature monuments, Indian handicrafts, and park memorabilia near the Mini India Zone.
Visitor Tips

Make the Most of Your Visit

✦
🌤️
Best Season: October to March
Delhi's pleasant winter (15–25°C) makes this the ideal season. The green landscape is at its lushest, and the cooler temperature makes long walks enjoyable. Avoid May–June when temperatures can exceed 45°C.
⏰
Arrive Early on Weekdays
Reach by 9:00 AM on weekdays to experience the park at its quietest. Weekends (especially Sundays) are very busy — arrive before 8:00 AM or after 5:00 PM.
🌙
Evening Visits Are Magical
The park transforms after sunset. Illuminated monument replicas, the evening Musical Fountain show (7:00 PM & 8:30 PM), and the ambient lighting of the Skywalk create a breathtaking experience. Plan an evening visit at least once.
👟
Wear Comfortable Footwear
The park spans 220 acres and a full-day visit can involve 8–12 km of walking. Comfortable shoes are a must. Avoid formal shoes, high heels, or new footwear for a park visit.
💧
Carry Water
While water is available at the park's kiosks, carrying a personal water bottle (especially in summer) is strongly recommended. Avoid plastic; refill stations are available.
📸
Photography Tips
Best photography light is at golden hour (7:00–8:30 AM and 5:30–7:00 PM). The Mini India Zone's monument reflections in the Sarovar are best captured in the morning. The Skywalk offers unique aerial shots.
Park Rules

Visitor Guidelines

RuleDetails
🚫 No LitteringUse designated bins. Park has zero-plastic policy in key zones.
🌿 No Plant DamageDo not pluck flowers, damage trees, or disturb the ecological zones.
🚬 No SmokingSmoking is strictly prohibited inside the park premises.
🍶 No AlcoholAlcohol is not permitted inside the park.
🐾 PetsSmall pets may be allowed in designated areas. Leash required. Verify at entry.
📷 PhotographyPersonal photography is freely permitted. Commercial shoots require prior DDA approval.
🏊 No SwimmingSwimming in Vandana Sarovar and all water bodies is strictly prohibited.
🎭 Dress CodeModest, respectful clothing expected in cultural and heritage zones.
🧒 Child SafetyChildren under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.
🆔 ID RequiredCarry a valid government-issued ID. Required for ticket purchase and adventure zone activities.
